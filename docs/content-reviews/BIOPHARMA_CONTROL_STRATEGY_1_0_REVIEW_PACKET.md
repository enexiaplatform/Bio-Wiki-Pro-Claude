- biologics CMC or product-quality reviewer;

- analytical development or bioassay reviewer;
- quality or regulatory CMC reviewer.

## Critical review checklist

- [ ] The lesson does not imply that final-product testing creates product quality.
- [ ] Candidate CQAs and process parameters are presented as product-specific evidence conclusions, not universal lists.
- [ ] Platform knowledge is separated from product-specific evidence.
- [ ] Cell-substrate, upstream, downstream, formulation, analytical-control, stability, and lifecycle links are scientifically coherent.
- [ ] Technology-transfer readiness, execution authorization, analytical transfer, comparability, validation, filing, routine readiness, and acceptance remain distinct.
- [ ] Supplier approval, material qualification, incoming release, process suitability, single-use-system qualification, comparability, change acceptance, and continuity remain distinct.
- [ ] Clone selection, bank establishment/release/use, characterization, genetic/phenotypic stability, production-age, comparability and batch disposition remain distinct.
- [ ] Identity, coding-sequence, productivity or one adventitious-agent result is not presented as proof of total cell-substrate suitability.
- [ ] Process design, qualification, PPQ, validation conclusion, routine authorization, CPV signal assessment, batch disposition, change approval and regulatory acceptance remain distinct.
- [ ] Statistical signals and specification signals are not collapsed into an automatic process-state or batch decision.
- [ ] Unchanged material names, grades, part numbers, drawings, CoAs or specifications are not presented as proof of unchanged function or product impact.
- [ ] Vendor extractables data and sterilization certificates are not presented as product-specific leachables, integrity, compatibility, or sterility-assurance conclusions.
- [ ] Q5E comparability is not reduced to release-specification conformance.
- [ ] Q5E scope is distinguished from biosimilarity and regional change classification.
- [ ] The comparability lesson avoids universal batch counts, statistical limits, study panels, and reporting categories.
- [ ] The fictional monoclonal-antibody example contains no disguised benchmark, acceptance criterion, or guaranteed outcome.
- [ ] Advanced modalities are explicitly outside the current evidence claim.
- [ ] Product, regional, filing, approved-procedure, validation, and accountable-review boundaries are visible.
- [ ] Quiz rationale and options do not create unsupported universal rules.
- [ ] All cited source versions and locators remain current at review time.

Any unchecked critical item blocks promotion regardless of numerical score.

## Usability acceptance

- [ ] A reader can name one cross-functional decision before collecting data.
- [ ] The workflow produces a product-process evidence map rather than a generic CQA/CPP list.
- [ ] Evidence held, platform knowledge, hypothesis, contradiction, and missing evidence remain distinguishable.
- [ ] Each material gap can be assigned to a reviewer and closure decision.
- [ ] The workflow can be completed without confidential formulation, process setting, patient, or batch data in Atlas analytics.
