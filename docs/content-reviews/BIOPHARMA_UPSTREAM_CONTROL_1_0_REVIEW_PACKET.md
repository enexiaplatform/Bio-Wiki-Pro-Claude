# Biopharma Upstream Control 1.0 Review Packet

**Document ID:** LSA-PRO-BIO-UPSTREAM-REVIEW-001

**Content version:** 1.0.0-review

**Review status:** editorial-reviewed; not SME-approved

**Risk level:** high

## Review scope

This packet covers:

- `biopharma-upstream-process-control.en.mdx`;
- the blank Biopharma Upstream Control Evidence Map workbook;
- the completed fictional workbook example;
- the guide, source map, quiz v2, workflow placement, and quality metadata.

It does not approve a product-specific CQA or CPP designation, material
attribute, operating range, control strategy, design space, validation plan,
comparability conclusion, specification, regulatory filing, or batch decision.

## Source basis

| Source ID | Primary review question |
| --- | --- |
| ICH-Q8-R2 | Are material-attribute and process-parameter links framed as product-specific evidence questions rather than universal lists? |
| ICH-Q11 | Is biotechnology drug-substance development and control-strategy scope represented accurately? |
| ICH-Q5D | Are cell-substrate and cell-banking statements limited to the applicable scope? |
| ICH-Q6B | Are characterization, specification, and total-control-strategy roles kept distinct? |
| ICH-Q9-R1 | Is risk management used to improve decisions without manufacturing evidence or criticality? |
| ICH-Q10 | Are change, knowledge, and lifecycle governance connected to accountable ownership? |
| FDA-PROCESS-VALIDATION-2011 | Are process design, qualification, and continued verification described as a lifecycle without implying a product-specific validation conclusion? |

Regional implementation, approved filings, licensed compendial content, and
site procedures must be checked separately before regulated use.

## Required reviewer roles

The package cannot become `sme-reviewed` until the applicable scope has
documented review from:

- upstream process development or MSAT;
- analytical development or product-quality sciences;
- raw-material sciences or cell-line ownership where applicable;
- quality or change-control ownership;
- regulatory CMC when the decision may affect a filing or registered control.

One person may cover more than one role only when appointment and competence
evidence supports that coverage. Atlas must not name or imply an SME before
permission and appointment evidence exist.

## Critical review checklist

- [ ] The lesson and workbook do not present a universal CQA, CPP, material-attribute, or performance-indicator list.
- [ ] Product attributes, material attributes, process parameters, performance indicators, and process outputs remain distinct.
- [ ] Platform knowledge and mechanistic plausibility are not presented as product-specific proof.
- [ ] Association, designed-study support, multi-source support, and contradictory evidence remain distinguishable.
- [ ] A process signal with unchanged tested attributes does not become evidence of no product impact.
- [ ] Every numeric range or criterion requires a controlled product/site reference; the package supplies no generic numeric recommendation.
- [ ] The workbook does not convert an observed range, equipment capability, development range, proven acceptable range, or design space into another category.
- [ ] Scale, site, vessel, sensor, control-system, raw-material, and process-version boundaries remain explicit.
- [ ] The fictional CHO example contains no disguised benchmark, approved range, acceptance criterion, or guaranteed conclusion.
- [ ] The workbook's formula-driven status cannot become review-ready while required decision fields or records are incomplete.
- [ ] Open or contradictory evidence remains visible and assignable to an accountable role.
- [ ] Advanced modalities and materially different fermentation systems are outside the current evidence claim.
- [ ] All source versions, locators, and regional boundaries remain current at review time.

Any unchecked critical item blocks promotion regardless of numerical score.

## Workbook usability acceptance

- [ ] The blank workbook opens with eight named sheets and no formula error.
- [ ] The blank workbook reports `Evidence required` before a decision and evidence are entered.
- [ ] A user can frame one product/process decision before entering parameter relationships.
- [ ] Every relationship can reference affected product attributes, evidence, scope, control placement, lifecycle trigger, owner, and review state.
- [ ] Missing fields, weak evidence, unresolved evidence, missing range provenance, and incomplete review produce visible attention flags.
- [ ] The completed fictional example remains `Qualified review required` while its open evidence and actions remain unresolved.
- [ ] Data validation, filters, frozen headers, instructions, and protection are usable in the target desktop spreadsheet application.
- [ ] The guide PDF and both workbooks open and match the package manifest.

## Open evidence and product backlog

The following are deliberately unresolved and must not be inferred from this
package:

1. product-specific upstream criticality and causal relationships;
2. acceptable operating, action, alert, or process-performance ranges;
3. modality-specific control logic beyond the stated protein/polypeptide scope;
4. scale-up, scale-down, mixing, mass-transfer, sensor, and equipment equivalence;
5. material variability and supplier-change conclusions;
6. downstream clearance, formulation, stability, and drug-product consequences;
7. validation, comparability, regulatory, and batch-disposition decisions;
8. evidence from real users demonstrating that the workbook changes a decision or produces a reusable controlled record.
