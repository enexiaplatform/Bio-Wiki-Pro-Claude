## Critical boundaries

Do not promote this package as an analytical method, validation or transfer
conclusion, statistical rule, CPP decision, OOS disposition, CAPA effectiveness
conclusion, product release decision or change authorization.

Confirm that the lesson and Decision Lab preserve signal reproducibility,
measurement/process/product context, competing hypotheses, affected scope,
correction, CAPA, effectiveness, change and knowledge transfer as distinct
objects. Exercise completion must never imply investigation, CAPA or change
closure.
