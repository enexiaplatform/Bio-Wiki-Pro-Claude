## Critical review checklist

- [ ] Cell-line development, clone selection, bank establishment, bank release,
  adventitious-agent assessment, genetic/phenotypic stability, process
  suitability, comparability, authorized use and batch disposition remain
  distinct decisions.
- [ ] Origin, source, culture, manipulation, selection, cloning, adaptation,
  transfer and bank history retain gaps rather than replacing them with inference.
- [ ] Construct evidence is not treated as proof of transcription, translation,
  phenotype, post-translational attributes or total product quality.
- [ ] Clone productivity is not presented as a complete selection rationale.
- [ ] Bank manufacture completion, label identity and inventory count are not
  presented as bank release or authorization to use.
- [ ] Characterization methods state material, timing, capability, limitations,
  contradiction and applicable reviewer.
- [ ] Production-age stability integrates exact culture-age traceability,
  construct/expression, phenotype, process and product evidence.
- [ ] No universal test panel, passage/production-age limit, sampling plan,
  criterion, qualification-lot count or regulatory category is supplied.
- [ ] The fictional CHO case contains no disguised real sequence, bank, process,
  batch, method, limit, benchmark or outcome.
- [ ] All cited source versions and locators remain current at review time.

Any unchecked critical item blocks promotion regardless of numerical score.

## Workbook usability acceptance

- [ ] The blank workbook fails closed as `Evidence required`.
- [ ] The fictional workbook is structurally complete but remains `Qualified review required`.
- [ ] Lineage/construct, clone, bank hierarchy, manufacture/storage,
  characterization, production-age stability, change, evidence and actions are
  traceable by stable IDs.
- [ ] Formula checks expose missing evidence, review and actions without
  converting completeness into approval.
- [ ] Filters, frozen headers, validation, instructions, sources, limitations
  and reviewer/sign-off fields work in a supported spreadsheet application.
- [ ] No confidential cell-line, construct, sequence, bank, batch, product or
  patient data appears in the distributed fictional example.

## Open evidence and product backlog

2. a real permissioned cell-line or replacement-bank case with implementation,
   corrections and actual process/product outcomes;
3. product-specific characterization, stability and comparability evidence;
4. modality-specific treatment of non-CHO and non-protein systems;
5. confirmation of regional/product-specific implementation before controlled use.
