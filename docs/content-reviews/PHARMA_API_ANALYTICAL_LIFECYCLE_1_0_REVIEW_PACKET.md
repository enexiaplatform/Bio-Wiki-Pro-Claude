# Pharma API Analytical & Lifecycle — 1.0 Review Packet

Status: `under-review`  
Content version: `1.0.0-review`

## Review scope

Review the lesson, guide, blank workbook and fictional example for analytical-science accuracy, method lifecycle logic, stability boundaries, transfer usability, quality governance and regulatory CMC limitations.

## Required reviewers

- API analytical development / method lifecycle
- QC laboratory and data integrity
- Statistics or measurement science
- Stability / product quality
- Validation / technology transfer
- Quality Unit
- Regulatory CMC

## Critical fail conditions

- Precision, specification conformance or passing transfer data presented as proof of fitness or approval
- Invented method, system-suitability criterion, validation, stability, specification, shelf-life or filing outcome
- Missing intended-use, matrix, source, limitation, evidence locator, action owner or change impact
- Workbook formula error, broken required sheet or status implying approval

## Acceptance evidence required

Record reviewer role, scope, date, corrections, unresolved issues and disposition. Product-level promotion remains blocked until score ≥85, critical fails are closed, qualified review is recorded and a permissioned real method lifecycle case demonstrates decision usefulness.
