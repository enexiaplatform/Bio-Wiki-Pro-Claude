# Biopharma Formulation & Stability 1.0 Review Packet

**Document ID:** LSA-PRO-BIOPHARMA-FORM-STAB-REVIEW-001

**Content version:** 1.0.0-review

**Review status:** editorial-reviewed; not SME-approved

**Risk level:** high

## Review scope

This packet covers:

- `biopharma-formulation-fill-finish-stability.en.mdx`;
- the formulation and stability workflow content registered in Atlas Pro;
- the blank and fictional Biopharma Formulation & Stability Evidence Map workbooks;
- its four-question quiz v2, content-quality metadata, sources, and limitations.

It does not approve a formulation, process, filter, container, device, study,
storage or shipping condition, shelf life, aseptic process, comparability
conclusion, validation decision, regulatory filing, or batch disposition.

## Source basis

| Source ID | Primary review question |
| --- | --- |
| ICH-Q5C | Is the biologics-specific stability logic faithful to product, material, method, study, storage, and lifecycle scope? |
| ICH-Q1A-R2 | Are general study and data-package statements appropriately bounded and used with Q5C? |
| ICH-Q1B | Are light-exposure and photostability statements conditional rather than universal? |
| ICH-Q6B | Are characterization, degradation, potency, purity, and specification roles distinguished? |
| ICH-Q5E | Does change wording avoid equating release conformance or analytical identity with a complete conclusion? |
| EU-GMP-ANNEX1-2022 | Are sterile fill-finish and container-integrity statements limited to the applicable manufacturing scope? |
| FDA-CONTAINER-CLOSURE-1999 | Are system suitability and product-package evidence distinguished from component conformance? |
| FDA-CCS-VIAL-STOPPER-2024 | Is the change example bounded to glass-vial/stopper scope and free of invented reporting categories? |
| ICH-Q8-R2 / ICH-Q9-R1 / ICH-Q10 | Are development, risk, knowledge, and lifecycle statements used without manufacturing evidence or approval claims? |

The draft consolidated ICH Q1 guideline is a revision watch item, not an effective
replacement in this release. Regional implementation and product-specific filing
requirements must be checked separately before regulated use.

## Required reviewer roles

The package cannot become `sme-reviewed` until applicable scope has documented
review from:

- formulation development or product-quality sciences;
- fill-finish/MSAT or process engineering;
- analytical development and stability sciences;
- container-closure or device engineering where applicable;
- sterile-product quality/validation where applicable;
- regulatory CMC and quality-unit review.

One person may cover more than one role only when appointment and competence
evidence supports that coverage. Public copy must not name or imply an SME before
permission and appointment evidence exist.

## Critical review checklist

- [ ] Q5C is used as the biologics-specific stability basis and no shelf life, condition, interval, batch count, test panel, or acceptance criterion is invented.
- [ ] Current effective Q1A(R2)/Q1B content is distinguished from draft consolidated Q1 revision activity.
- [ ] Degradation pathways are product-specific hypotheses or observations, not universal conclusions.
- [ ] Formulation components are linked to evidence and limitations rather than copied as a platform recipe.
- [ ] Sterility assurance and molecular stability remain distinct evidence systems.
- [ ] Container component conformance is not presented as complete system suitability or integrity.
- [ ] Accelerated and stress evidence is not represented as a direct substitute for justified long-term evidence.
- [ ] Stability-indicating analytical coverage includes method and sample limitations.
- [ ] The fictional case contains no disguised composition, process parameter, storage condition, limit, shelf life, or regulatory outcome.
- [ ] Q5E change language avoids automatic equivalence or comparability conclusions.
- [ ] Advanced modalities and out-of-scope presentations are explicit.
- [ ] Product, route, process, presentation, region, filing, and qualified-review boundaries are visible.
- [ ] Quiz rationale and options create no unsupported universal rule.
- [ ] All source versions and locators remain current at review time.

Any unchecked critical item blocks promotion regardless of numerical score.

## Workbook usability acceptance

- [ ] All ten sheets open and retain validation, formulas, source URLs, limitations, and status formatting.
- [ ] Blank workbook status is `Evidence required`.
- [ ] The fictional example status is `Qualified review required`, not approved or review-ready.
- [ ] Quality/degradation, formulation, process/fill, stability studies, evidence, and actions remain separately traceable.
- [ ] No blank required field can produce `Ready for qualified review`.
- [ ] No generic formulation, concentration, parameter, condition, schedule, limit, shelf life, or filing category is embedded.
- [ ] Every material unresolved record is visible in the decision summary and assigned action.
- [ ] The workbook remains an educational working file rather than a controlled protocol, study report, change record, or filing.

## Open evidence and product backlog

1. Qualified review and recorded corrections across all applicable roles.
2. A permissioned real formulation, fill-finish, container, shipping, or stability change case.
3. Verification that workbook controls remain intact in the supported Excel environments.
4. Product-specific evidence showing whether the workflow improves a real decision without exposing confidential data.
5. Revision review when the consolidated ICH Q1 guideline becomes final and regionally implemented.
