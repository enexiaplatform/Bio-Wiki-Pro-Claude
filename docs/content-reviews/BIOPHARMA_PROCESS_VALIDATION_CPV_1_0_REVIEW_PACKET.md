## Critical review checklist

- [ ] Process design, enabling qualification, PPQ execution, validation
  conclusion, authorization to proceed, batch disposition, CPV signal review,
  change approval and regulatory acceptance remain distinct decisions.
- [ ] No fixed batch/run count, universal sampling plan, statistical method,
  control limit, capability threshold, acceptance criterion, monitoring
  frequency or revalidation trigger is supplied.
- [ ] Product/process relationships preserve direct evidence, platform
  knowledge, hypotheses, contradiction, scope and uncertainty.
- [ ] Facility, equipment, utility, automation/data, method, material,
  personnel and procedural readiness are not collapsed into one status.
- [ ] PPQ design explicitly addresses representativeness, genealogy,
  predefined evaluation, deviations, missing data and protocol changes.
- [ ] CPV records baseline population, stratification, method/version,
  threshold source, data integrity, latency, ownership and escalation.
- [ ] Statistical signals and specification signals are assessed separately;
  neither automatically supplies root cause, validation status or disposition.
- [ ] Capability metrics are not interpreted without distribution,
  measurement-system, stability, independence, stratification and limit review.
- [ ] Change assessment covers qualification, validation, comparability,
  regulatory and CPV-baseline impact before implementation.
- [ ] The fictional case contains no disguised real parameter, range, method,
  limit, batch, product, benchmark or outcome.
- [ ] All source versions and locators remain current at review time.

Any unchecked critical item blocks promotion regardless of numerical score.

## Workbook usability acceptance

- [ ] The blank workbook fails closed as `Evidence required`.
- [ ] The fictional workbook is structurally complete but remains `Qualified review required`.
- [ ] Process, qualification, PPQ, CPV plan/data, signal, evidence and actions
  are traceable by stable IDs and controlled versions.
- [ ] Formula checks distinguish incomplete evidence, statistical signals,
  specification signals, review and open actions without creating approval.
- [ ] Filters/frozen headers, categorical validation, sources, limitations and
  reviewer/sign-off fields work in a supported spreadsheet application.
- [ ] No confidential product, process, batch, method, supplier or patient data
  appears in the distributed fictional example.
