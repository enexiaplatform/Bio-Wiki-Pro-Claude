## Review scope

- `biopharma-raw-ancillary-materials-control.en.mdx`;
- the guide, blank workbook and fictional completed example in
  `biopharma-materials-control`;
- source, quiz-v2, paid-asset and Biopharma coverage metadata;
- Academy, toolkit and Biopharma workflow integration.

This packet does not approve a supplier, material, specification, test-reduction
strategy, single-use system, E&L assessment, validation, comparability plan,
change, filing, batch or Biologics Domain Pack.

## Source basis

| Source ID | Primary review question |
| --- | --- |
| ICH-Q11 | Are material attributes and controls connected to the defined drug-substance process without universal criteria? |
| ICH-Q9-R1 | Are uncertainty, subjectivity and supply-chain risks explicit rather than hidden behind a score? |
| ICH-Q10 | Are supplier, outsourced-activity, knowledge and lifecycle change responsibilities preserved? |
| ICH-Q5A-R2 | Are biological-origin/adventitious-agent questions within scope and free of blanket safety conclusions? |
| ICH-Q5E | Is material-change comparability kept distinct from conformance and identity? |
| EU-GMP-ANNEX1-2022 | Are contamination-control and single-use statements limited to applicable sterile-manufacturing scope? |
| WHO-TRS-996-ANNEX3 | Are biological-product GMP material/source statements bounded by product and national implementation? |
| FDA-QUALITY-AGREEMENTS-2016 | Are owner/contractor responsibilities and change communication preserved? |

## Required reviewer roles

- raw-material or material-science owner;
- supplier quality and supply-chain/change-notification owner;
- process development/MSAT and manufacturing;
- process engineering and single-use-system specialist;
- extractables/leachables and toxicology specialist where applicable;
- microbiology, contamination-control and viral-safety specialist where applicable;
- analytical development/statistics and QC where applicable;
- validation, quality unit and regulatory CMC.

## Critical review checklist

- [ ] Supplier approval, material qualification, incoming release, process
  suitability, SUS qualification, comparability, change acceptance and supply
  continuity remain distinct decisions.
- [ ] The package does not imply that unchanged name, grade, part number,
  drawing, CoA or specification establishes functional equivalence.
- [ ] Functional attributes and failure modes are product/process specific and
  are not converted into a universal critical-material list.
- [ ] Supplier, manufacturing/test site, sub-tier source, processing,
  packaging, sterilization, distribution and change notification are traceable.
- [ ] Vendor extractables data is not treated as a product-specific leachables
  or toxicology conclusion.
- [ ] A sterilization certificate is not treated as proof of configured-system
  integrity, compatibility, sterility assurance or process suitability.
- [ ] Biological-origin, adventitious-agent, TSE and viral-safety wording is
  applied only where relevant and does not manufacture a blanket conclusion.
- [ ] The fictional CHO example contains no disguised benchmark, test panel,
  numerical criterion, toxicology limit, study design or approval.
- [ ] Product, process, presentation, site, market, filing, validation and
  accountable-review boundaries are visible.
- [ ] All cited source versions and locators remain current at review time.

Any unchecked critical item blocks promotion regardless of numerical score.

## Workbook usability acceptance

- [ ] The blank workbook fails closed as `Evidence required`.
- [ ] The fictional workbook is structurally complete but remains `Qualified review required`.
- [ ] Material/contact, supplier chain, functional attributes, SUS configuration,
  change impacts, evidence and actions are traceable by stable IDs.
- [ ] Formula checks expose missing evidence, review and actions without
  converting completeness into approval.
- [ ] Lists, filters, frozen headers, instructions, sources, limitations and
  reviewer/sign-off fields work in a supported spreadsheet application.
- [ ] No confidential product, supplier, batch or patient data appears in the
  distributed fictional example.

## Open evidence and product backlog

1. qualified review across the applicable roles above;
2. a real permissioned raw/ancillary-material or SUS change case with
   implementation, corrections and actual process/product outcomes;
3. product- and process-specific E&L/toxicology evidence where relevant;
4. deeper cell-line-development and genetic-stability decision coverage;
5. confirmation of regional and product-specific implementation before
   controlled use.
