# Decision Package Full-Content Audit

Audited: 2026-08-11

Scope: the twelve Atlas End-to-End Content Intelligence Decision Packages across Biopharma, Pharma/API, Drug Product and cross-product evidence governance.

## Full-package standard

A package is editorially complete only when all of the following are repository-backed and contract-valid:

1. Canonical public Decision Package route with decision question, discovery prompts, applicability, limitations, source versions, licensing boundary and four-product handoff.
2. Canonical Pro deep lesson with at least 1,000 body words, controlled source map, decision boundary, worked fictional example, working-asset link and at least three assessment questions.
3. Typed learning flow with at least three knowledge units, five gated workflow phases, three evidence/study activities, three reasoning checks and four completion criteria.
4. Package-specific fictional Decision Lab with starting evidence, three event rounds, learner tasks, evidence records, review gates, four expected artifacts, debrief and explicit synthetic boundary.
5. Bounded workflow or tool linked to the package.
6. Working asset, fictional completed example and repository-backed review packet.
7. Source IDs/versions, reviewer roles, applicability, limitations, stage mappings and Public/Pro/Quality Lab/Career bindings.
8. Guest/Pro entitlement protection: detailed flow and Decision Lab bodies remain server-gated.

This standard measures editorial and structural completeness. It does not mean `sme-reviewed`, Domain Pack verification, validated use, regulatory acceptance or Gate 1 completion.

## Gaps closed in this audit

- Expanded all four Drug Product lessons from framework summaries into deep lessons with evidence objects, five decision gates, study architecture and failure modes.
- Added a dedicated cross-product Pro lesson covering reproducible signals, data/measurement/process context, statistics, investigation, affected scope, correction, CAPA, effectiveness, change and knowledge transfer.
- Expanded the two shortest API lessons past the canonical depth gate with controlled review handoffs.
- Upgraded all four Drug Product blank assets from single-row registers to five-record workflows.
- Expanded all four Drug Product fictional examples through baseline, evidence challenge, study/review and lifecycle handoff.
- Rebound the cross-cutting package to its dedicated canonical lesson while retaining Data Integrity, OOS and statistics as supporting resources.
- Added automated depth, section, assessment, asset-structure and fictional-example gates.

## External gates intentionally open

- All learning flows and Decision Labs remain `specialist-review-required`.
- No package is SME-approved through this audit.
- No new Compiler Domain Pack is verified or executable.
- No real permissioned case, qualified specialist disposition or Gate 1 engagement is created by content completion.
- Product/site formulas, methods, operating ranges, specifications, acceptance criteria, validation conclusions, disposition and regulatory decisions remain outside Atlas content authority.
