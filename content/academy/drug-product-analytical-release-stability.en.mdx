# Drug product analytical, release and stability lifecycle

## Decision question

What evidence supports the chain from analytical purpose and capability to
release, stability, packaging and lifecycle decisions for this drug product?

## Applicability and do-not-use boundary

This is a cross-dosage-form framework with a synthetic OSD planning example.
It does not provide a product-specific method, system-suitability criterion,
specification, dissolution limit, stability condition, shelf life, packaging
qualification or release decision. Confirm applicability with analytical,
QC, stability, packaging, quality and regulatory CMC reviewers.

## Controlled source map

| Source | Decision use | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ICH Q1A(R2) | Frame stability evidence and lifecycle questions | It does not assign shelf life without product data |
| ICH Q2(R2) | Connect analytical procedure performance to intended purpose | It does not create a product specification |
| ICH Q6A | Link specifications to product and process knowledge | It does not supply a universal limit |
| ICH Q14 | Structure analytical procedure development and lifecycle | It does not replace validation or transfer |
| FDA container-closure guidance | Frame packaging-system evidence questions | It does not establish product-specific integrity or release criteria |

## Evidence chain

1. State the product/presentation, decision, sample context and intended use.
2. Link each attribute or performance question to method purpose, capability,
   reference/material status, data integrity and known limitations.
3. Keep release, stability, packaging and change evidence connected while
   preserving observed signals and competing hypotheses.
4. Record the accountable review needed before any method, specification,
   stability or release conclusion is adopted.

## Discovery questions

- What intended use, product presentation and decision does each analytical procedure support?
- Which method-performance, reference-material, stability or packaging observations could change interpretation?
- Which signals should connect release, stability, investigation and change-control review?
- Where does evidence stop short of a dosage-form-specific or product-specific conclusion?

## Decision table

| Evidence position | Record | Bounded next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Method is fit for its stated purpose but lifecycle evidence is open | Purpose, evidence version and limitation | Assign analytical, QC and quality review |
| Release or stability signal is discrepant | Signal, sample context and competing explanations | Open investigation without assuming root cause |
| Packaging observation affects interpretation | Container-closure context and affected claim | Request packaging, stability and regulatory CMC review |
