# Performance-to-release decision guide

## Decision question

What evidence supports the product-performance, release, stability and
packaging control chain for a defined drug product presentation?

## Bounded workflow

1. Freeze product, dosage form, presentation, intended use and decision owner.
2. Link each performance or release decision to method purpose and capability.
3. Separate release observations, stability observations, packaging evidence,
   product hypotheses and source limitations.
4. Record investigation triggers and change impact without inventing limits or
   shelf life.
5. Define the qualified analytical, stability, packaging, quality and regulatory
   review required before any controlled conclusion.

## Discovery questions

- What intended use and decision does each analytical procedure support?
- Which performance, stability or packaging observation could change interpretation?
- What investigation, change-control or lifecycle signal must remain connected?

## Controlled source map

| Source | Decision use | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ICH-Q1A-R2 | Frame stability evidence | Does not assign shelf life without product data |
| ICH-Q2-R2 | Connect procedure performance to intended purpose | Does not create a specification |
| ICH-Q6A | Link specifications to product/process knowledge | Does not supply a universal limit |
| ICH-Q14 | Structure analytical procedure lifecycle | Does not replace validation or transfer |
| FDA-CONTAINER-CLOSURE-1999 | Frame packaging-system evidence questions | Does not establish product-specific integrity or release criteria |
