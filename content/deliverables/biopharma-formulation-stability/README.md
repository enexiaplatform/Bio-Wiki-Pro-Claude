# Biopharma Formulation & Stability Evidence Map - Start Here

This package helps a cross-functional biopharma team connect degradation
pathways, formulation hypotheses, process and fill-finish exposure, the complete
container-closure presentation, stability-indicating methods, study evidence,
changes, commitments, and accountable review.

**Package version:** 1.0.0-review

**Review status:** editorial-reviewed; not SME-approved

**Decision owner:** the authorized product, formulation, fill-finish, stability,
analytical, quality, validation, and regulatory owners - not Atlas

## Package contents

1. `biopharma-formulation-stability-guide.pdf` - decision workflow, evidence
   model, source basis, and controlled-use boundaries.
2. `biopharma-formulation-stability-v1.xlsx` - blank formula-driven working file
   with validations, evidence register, attention flags, sources, and limitations.
3. `biopharma-formulation-stability-v1-fictional-example.xlsx` - completed
   fictional CHO protein example preserving unresolved stopper, hold/agitation,
   particle, aggregate, analytical, container, and stability questions.

## Recommended order

1. Confirm that the product, route, presentation, and process are in scope.
2. Frame one formulation, fill-finish, container, shipping, storage, or change decision.
3. Map product-quality attributes and proposed degradation pathways.
4. Record formulation components as product-specific hypotheses with evidence.
5. Map actual process, fill-finish, hold, handling, and shipping exposures.
6. Build the study matrix and stability-indicating analytical coverage.
7. Preserve contradictions, limitations, commitments, and lifecycle triggers.
8. Resolve every `Incomplete`, `Evidence required`, or `Review required` flag.
9. Obtain qualified cross-functional review and transfer approved content into the site's controlled system.

## Important boundary

The package supplies no platform formulation or generic operating range; in
particular, it provides no generic operating range for product use. It supplies
no component equivalence, process parameter, hold time, storage or
shipping condition, test schedule, specification, acceptance criterion, shelf
life, validation conclusion, comparability conclusion, filing category, or batch
decision. Every product-specific claim requires controlled provenance and
qualified review.
