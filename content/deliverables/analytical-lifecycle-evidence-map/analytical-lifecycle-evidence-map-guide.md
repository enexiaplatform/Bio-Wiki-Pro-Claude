# Analytical Lifecycle Evidence Map — Guide

Status: `editorial-reviewed` · Version: `1.0.0-review`

## Decision question

What evidence must remain connected from analytical purpose through procedure
performance, validation, transfer and lifecycle change before a result can
support its intended decision?

## Applicability and do-not-use boundary

Use this pack to structure cross-product analytical questions for a named
product or material, matrix, attribute, intended use, procedure version, site
and lifecycle event. Do not use it to select a universal method, prescribe
performance criteria, reproduce licensed pharmacopoeial text, approve a
specification, conclude validation or authorize transfer, filing or release.

## Controlled source map

| Source ID | Use in this pack | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ICH-Q14 | Analytical procedure development and lifecycle framing | Does not supply a product ATP, method or acceptance criteria |
| ICH-Q2-R2 | Performance-characteristic and validation evidence questions | Does not establish a validation design or conclusion |
| ICH-Q9-R1 | Risk-based evidence depth and uncertainty review | Risk scoring cannot replace missing evidence |
| ICH-Q10 | Change, knowledge and lifecycle governance | Does not approve a site procedure or change |

## Workflow

1. Freeze one decision, product/material, matrix, attribute and intended use.
2. State the analytical target profile as a reviewable planning claim, not an
   approved specification.
3. Separate procedure design, instrument/platform, sample preparation,
   reference, reagent and data-system evidence.
4. Map each performance claim to a source, version, locator, applicability and
   limitation.
5. Preserve contradictions, missing evidence and measurement-system questions.
6. Connect validation, transfer, routine monitoring and change triggers without
   treating one passing study as full lifecycle fitness.
7. Record accountable reviewers, the permitted decision and owned next action.

## Discovery questions

- Which result and downstream decision define the intended analytical use?
- What product, matrix, range, procedure version, site and market boundaries
  limit the evidence?
- Which performance claims are observed, inferred, planned or still missing?
- What change would reopen development, validation, transfer or specification
  review?

## Limitations

This pack is educational decision support. Qualified product-specific review,
current source verification, controlled procedures and site evidence remain
required. The fictional example is not a precedent or transferable acceptance
basis.
