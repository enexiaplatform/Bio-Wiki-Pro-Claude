# Unit-operation and scale-up decision guide

## Decision question

Which development, equipment and process-control evidence is needed before an
OSD scale-up decision is reviewed?

## Bounded workflow

1. Freeze product, dosage form, process version, equipment train and intended
   decision.
2. Map dispensing, blending, granulation, drying, compression, coating or
   packaging as applicable; do not assume every operation applies.
3. Separate equipment facts, hold/sampling facts, process observations and
   hypotheses.
4. Link process signals to analytical capability, material attributes and
   investigation triggers.
5. Record the receiving-site and validation questions still requiring review.

## Discovery questions

- Which unit operations and process parameters are expected to influence the intended quality attributes?
- What equipment, scale, material and hold differences could make transfer assumptions unsafe?
- Which in-process observation or sampling trigger should reopen review?

## Controlled source map

| Source | Decision use | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ICH-Q8-R2 | Connect unit operations and process understanding | Does not provide a scale-up equation |
| ICH-Q9-R1 | Prioritize equipment, hold and sampling questions | Risk priority is not an acceptance limit |
| ICH-Q10 | Carry process knowledge into lifecycle governance | Does not authorize execution |
| FDA-PROCESS-VALIDATION-2011 | Structure lifecycle validation evidence | Does not prescribe a universal batch plan |

No universal unit-operation sequence, range, scale factor, batch count or
process-capability target is supplied.

## Next handoff

Carry open scale-up evidence into analytical/release, validation/transfer and
change-control review. Product-specific process decisions remain with qualified
process, engineering, manufacturing, statistics, validation and quality roles.
