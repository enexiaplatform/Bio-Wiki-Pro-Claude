# Pharma API Starting Material & Input Control Guide

## Purpose

Build a reviewable evidence chain from a proposed API starting-material boundary through the real supplier/sub-tier chain, material attributes, impurity linkage, specification and method basis, incoming controls, change assessment and accountable decision.

## Applicability and limitations

Apply to a defined chemically synthesized small-molecule API, route version, lifecycle stage and market. Do not generalize unchanged to another modality, route or supplier. This guide provides no material identity, synthesis, supplier approval, specification, test, limit, sampling plan, reduced-testing authorization, filing conclusion or batch disposition.

## Source basis

- `ICH-Q11` and `ICH-Q11-QA`: integrated starting-material selection and justification in drug-substance development.
- `ICH-Q7` and `ICH-Q7-QA`: API GMP, materials, suppliers, contractors and lifecycle clarification.
- `ICH-Q3A-R2`, `ICH-Q3C-R9`, `ICH-Q3D-R2`, `ICH-M7-R2`: applicable impurity questions without product-specific conclusions.
- `ICH-Q6A`, `ICH-Q2-R2`, `ICH-Q14`: specification and analytical capability.
- `ICH-Q9-R1`, `ICH-Q10`: risk, knowledge, outsourced activities and change governance.
- `FDA-QUALITY-AGREEMENTS-2016`: clear owner/contract-facility responsibilities; an agreement does not transfer away quality responsibility.

## Workflow

1. Freeze product, route, proposed boundary, sites, lifecycle stage, markets and Decision owner.
2. Record the integrated boundary rationale: structure contribution, material synthesis knowledge, impurity fate and downstream control opportunities.
3. Map broker, distributor, legal manufacturer, actual sites and sub-tier operations separately.
4. Connect material attributes and actual/potential impurities to downstream process and product risk.
5. Trace each specification/test proposal to its intended use, method capability, sampling and evidence.
6. Define receipt, quarantine, sampling, testing, CoA verification, release, storage and authorized-use ownership.
7. Record supplier qualification, audit/assessment, quality-agreement and change-notification evidence without converting them into approval.
8. Assess supplier/site/process/specification/method/packaging/transport changes, affected inventory, validation/comparability and filing questions.
9. Close evidence and actions only with qualified review; preserve unresolved items.

## Critical review checklist

- Is the route and proposed starting-material boundary frozen and versioned?
- Does the justification integrate multiple Q11 considerations rather than one shortcut?
- Is the actual manufacturer/site/sub-tier chain distinguished from commercial intermediaries?
- Are material attributes and impurities linked to route position, downstream fate and control opportunity?
- Does each specification element have an explicit risk and analytical-capability basis?
- Are receipt, release, reduced testing, storage and use decisions separately owned?
- Does the quality agreement match actual operations and retained responsibilities?
- Are changes assessed for existing inventory, forward lots, validation/comparability and filing impact?
- Are evidence locators, limitations, actions and reviewer decisions present?

## Workbook usability acceptance

The package must open with all 12 sheets, keep formulas visible, distinguish blank/example content, preserve source IDs and show no formula errors. Blank state is `Evidence required`; completion can only become `Qualified review required`.

## Open evidence and product backlog

Qualified synthetic-process, supplier-quality, analytical/QC, quality and regulatory CMC review is not recorded. No permissioned real starting-material justification or supplier-change case is available.
