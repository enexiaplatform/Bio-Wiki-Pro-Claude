## Three-round practice trace

1. **Identity and scope:** reconcile material, API, batch and sample versions.
   The exercise stops if a result cannot be attributed to the declared state.
2. **Mechanism and confounding:** record evidence for and against each
   explanation. The exercise does not rank an unsupported association as
   criticality.
3. **Bounded decision:** assign evidence owners, limitations, due dates and the
   next lifecycle handoff. Authorization remains open.
