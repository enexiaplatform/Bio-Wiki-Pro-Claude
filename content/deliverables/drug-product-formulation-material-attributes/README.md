# Drug Product Formulation & Material Attributes Evidence Map

Version: `1.0.0-review`
Status: `editorial-reviewed`
Worked example: synthetic oral solid dose (OSD) only

This pack separates formulation intent, API/excipient attributes,
manufacturability, product performance and presentation evidence. It does not
provide a formulation, recipe, design space, bioequivalence conclusion,
specification or regulatory precedent.
