# Formulation-to-attribute decision guide

## Decision question

What product-performance and manufacturability evidence connects formulation
intent to API/excipient attributes and the proposed presentation?

## Bounded workflow

1. Identify dosage form, route, intended performance and decision owner.
2. Separate product facts, material facts, development hypotheses and unknowns.
3. Map each attribute to method capability, manufacturing consequence and
   evidence source.
4. Record OSD-specific assumptions separately from facts that can generalize.
5. Define qualified review, unresolved evidence and the next lifecycle action.

## Discovery questions

- Which product intent, quality attributes and material versions are fixed?
- What mechanism connects each attribute hypothesis to manufacturability or product performance?
- Which observation would change the next study, escalation or lifecycle action?

## Controlled source map

| Source | Decision use | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ICH-Q8-R2 | Frame product and process understanding | Does not provide a formula or design space |
| ICH-Q9-R1 | Structure risk questions | Risk ranking does not replace evidence or approval |
| ICH-Q10 | Carry knowledge into lifecycle change | Does not authorize implementation |

## Boundary

This guide does not establish a formulation, excipient level, design space,
bioequivalence result, dissolution limit, stability condition or acceptance
criterion. Non-OSD dosage forms remain framework-only.

## Next handoff

Carry the evidence map into the unit-operation, analytical/release and lifecycle
packages. Keep the OSD example synthetic and route unresolved questions to
formulation, material-science, analytical, quality and regulatory CMC reviewers.
