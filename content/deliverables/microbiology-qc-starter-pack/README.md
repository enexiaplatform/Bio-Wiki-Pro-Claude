# Microbiology QC Starter Pack — Start Here

The flagship onboarding pack for a new QC microbiology analyst in a GMP facility —
and for the supervisor or trainer responsible for getting them qualified. It turns
the first 90 days from "shadow whoever is free" into a structured, evidence-based
qualification plan grounded in the real compendial tests.

## Who it is for

- New QC microbiology analysts in their first 90 days.
- Trainers and supervisors building or refreshing an onboarding program.
- Analysts moving into microbiology from another QC discipline who need the
  compendial landscape fast.

## What is inside

1. **Microbiology QC Starter Guide** (`microbiology-qc-starter-pack.md`, rendered to
   PDF) — the working handbook:
   - What a QC micro lab actually does (release testing, EM, water, media,
     validations) and the regulatory map in plain language.
   - The core test battery bench-deep: TAMC/TYMC per USP <61> (all four plating
     methods, neutralization, incubation, counting rules, cfu math), specified
     organisms per USP <62> with enrichment media and confirmation tests, and
     Burkholderia cepacia complex per USP <60>.
   - Sterility testing per USP <71>: membrane filtration vs direct inoculation,
     FTM/SCD, 14-day incubation, growth promotion and bacteriostasis/fungistasis.
   - Method suitability: neutralization validation and the 50–200% recovery
     criterion, with a fill-in suitability record.
   - Water testing, raw-material bioburden, and EM basics.
   - Aseptic technique, lab disciplines, and ALCOA+ documentation habits.
   - Ten beginner mistakes with consequences and prevention.
   - Your first OOS: exactly what to do and not do in the first hour.
   - A 40-term glossary and a weeks 1–12 learning roadmap.
2. **First 90 Days Checklist** (`micro-qc-first-90-days.csv`, opens in Excel) —
   24 week-by-week tasks across weeks 1–12, each with the evidence to show your
   trainer plus status, initials, and date columns. Three example rows show a
   completed pattern; replace them with your own dates and names.

## Recommended order of use

1. Analyst reads the Starter Guide sections 1–2 before day one; the rest in the
   first two weeks.
2. Trainer and analyst review the CSV together in week 1 and agree target dates.
3. Work the CSV weekly; every sign-off should point to a real record (training
   entry, co-signed run, qualification certificate).
4. Revisit guide sections 7–10 around weeks 8–10, once the analyst is handling real
   plates and real records.

## Important

General educational workflow and training template. Verify everything against the
current pharmacopeia editions (USP/Ph. Eur./JP), your approved SOPs, and your site
quality system. Not a validated method, not QA approval, and not a substitute for
formal GMP training or regulatory advice.
