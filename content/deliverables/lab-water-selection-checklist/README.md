# Lab Water Selection Checklist — Start Here

An Atlas Pro pack for choosing, justifying, specifying, and defending the
right water grade for every task in a pharmaceutical QC laboratory — from
potable feed water through Purified Water (PW), Highly Purified Water (HPW),
and Water for Injections (WFI), to sterile and analytical reagent waters.

Built for QC microbiologists, analytical chemists, QC/QA managers, and
validation or engineering staff who need an audit-ready answer to "which
water, why, and how is it controlled?"

## What's inside

1. **lab-water-selection-checklist.md** (renders to PDF) — the main working
   guide, eleven sections:
   - Purpose, scope, and the governing compendia (USP <1231>/<645>/<643>/<85>,
     Ph. Eur. 0008/0169/1927, EU GMP Annex 1 2022, WHO TRS, FDA 1993
     high-purity water inspection guide).
   - A grade-by-grade decision framework with a selection checklist.
   - Lab-use mapping (media prep, reagents, glassware rinse, BET, HPLC,
     dilution blanks, EM neutralizers) plus a fill-in Water Use Justification
     Record.
   - System design overview (pretreatment → RO/EDI → polishing → loop),
     including the design points inspectors probe.
   - The Phase 1/2/3 validation approach with sampling logic.
   - Routine monitoring: conductivity three-stage logic, microbial method
     rationale (R2A, membrane filtration, incubation), frequency, and how to
     set alert/action levels from Phase 3 data.
   - Excursion handling, sanitization options, and resampling rules.
   - Trending and annual review.
   - Inspector expectations and recurring real-world findings.
   - A worked example: selecting water grades for a new QC microbiology lab.
2. **water-grade-quick-reference.csv** (opens in Excel) — an eight-row
   reference across potable, PW (USP and Ph. Eur.), HPW, WFI, sterile WFI,
   analytical/reagent water, and Water for BET: compendial basis, typical
   use, conductivity, TOC, microbial and endotoxin limits, system type,
   monitoring frequency, and an example alert level.

## Recommended order of use

1. Read the disclaimer at the top of the main guide, then Section 1.
2. Work the grade-selection checklist (Section 2.8) for each task in your
   lab; record each decision in the fill-in Water Use Justification Record
   (Section 3.3) and get QA approval.
3. Use the CSV to sanity-check the specifications for your chosen grade
   against the current compendial edition.
4. If you own or use a water system, adapt Sections 5–8 into your site
   validation and monitoring SOPs.
5. Before an inspection, walk Section 9 with your actual records.
6. Use Section 10 as the model for your own decision documentation.

## Important

Educational working template. Verify every limit and practice against the
current editions of USP–NF and Ph. Eur., EU GMP Annex 1, applicable WHO
guidance, your registered specifications, your approved SOPs, and your
validated water system state. Site-specific values shown as examples are
illustrative only. This pack is not validated, not regulator-approved, and
not a substitute for QA approval or regulatory advice.
