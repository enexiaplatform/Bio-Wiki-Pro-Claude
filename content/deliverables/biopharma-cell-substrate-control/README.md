# Biopharma Cell Substrate & Bank Lifecycle Evidence Map — Start Here

This package helps a biologics team connect a defined host, expression
construct, clone and bank version to lineage, characterization, stability,
process/product evidence, lifecycle change and accountable review.

**Package version:** 1.0.0-review

**Review status:** editorial-reviewed

**Decision owner:** the authorized cell-substrate/bank/change owner and quality
unit, supported by cell-line development, molecular characterization,
cell-bank manufacturing, biosafety, virology/viral safety, process/MSAT,
analytical/product quality, statistics, validation and regulatory CMC — not
Atlas

## Package contents

1. `biopharma-cell-substrate-control-guide.pdf` — decision workflow, evidence
   model, sources, reviewer roles and controlled-use boundaries.
2. `biopharma-cell-substrate-control-v1.xlsx` — blank formula-driven working
   file for lineage/construct, clone development, bank hierarchy,
   manufacture/storage, characterization, stability/production age, change,
   evidence, actions and review.
3. `biopharma-cell-substrate-control-v1-fictional-example.xlsx` — completed
   fictional CHO replacement-working-bank example that preserves unresolved
   lineage, characterization, stability, comparability and use questions.

## Recommended order

1. Freeze host, parental line, construct, clone, MCB/WCB versions, process,
   intended use, lifecycle stage, change and decision authority.
2. Reconstruct origin, culture, manipulation, selection, cloning, adaptation,
   transfer and bank history; preserve undocumented intervals.
3. Link construct evidence to transcript, protein, phenotype, process and
   product evidence without treating one layer as proof of the others.
4. Preserve clone-selection attributes, methods, process context, trade-offs
   and discarded alternatives—not productivity alone.
5. Map bank genealogy, manufacture, storage, inventory, shipment, thaw/use,
   excursions, replacement and retirement.
6. Define each characterization/safety question, method capability, material,
   timing, limitation, contradiction and reviewer.
7. Reconcile genetic/phenotypic stability through the relevant production age
   with process/product evidence and analytical sensitivity.
8. Keep bank release, authorized use, comparability, validation, filing and
   batch disposition as separate decisions.
9. Resolve each `Incomplete`, `Evidence required` or `Review required` flag.
10. Transfer only authorized decisions to controlled site systems.

## Important boundary

The package supplies no host, vector, construct, clone-selection method,
culture condition, bank-manufacturing process, test panel, sampling plan,
production-age limit, passage limit, acceptance criterion, qualification lot
count, adventitious-agent conclusion, comparability conclusion, bank approval,
manufacturing authorization or batch disposition.

An intact coding sequence does not by itself establish cell-substrate stability
or unchanged product quality. Bank manufacture completion is not bank release
or authorization to use.

Every product-specific claim requires controlled provenance and qualified
review.
