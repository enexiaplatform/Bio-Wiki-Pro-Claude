# Biopharma Cell Substrate & Bank Lifecycle Evidence Map

**Version:** 1.0.0-review

**Status:** Under review

**Document purpose:** Educational decision and evidence-mapping support

## Decision this package supports

For a defined protein biologic, host and expression system, clone and cell-bank
version, production process, lifecycle stage and proposed use or change, what
lineage, construct, characterization, stability, process/product,
comparability and governance evidence must be resolved before authorized owners
can establish, use, replace or extend the cell substrate?

## Use when

- reconstructing cell-line and expression-construct provenance;
- reviewing clone-development and selection rationale;
- establishing or replacing a master or working cell bank;
- mapping cell-bank manufacture, storage, inventory, shipment, thaw/use,
  excursions and retirement;
- evaluating genetic/phenotypic stability through relevant production age;
- assessing a bank, site, storage, process or analytical lifecycle change.

## Do not use as

- a bank-manufacturing protocol, characterization/testing specification,
  sampling plan or stability protocol;
- a source of hosts, vectors, constructs, selection systems, culture
  conditions, test panels, production-age limits, passage limits or criteria;
- bank release, authorized use, validation, comparability, regulatory approval
  or batch disposition;
- proof that identity, coding sequence, phenotype, productivity or a negative
  adventitious-agent result alone establishes suitability;
- an unchanged template outside the exact cell-substrate and product scope.

## Workflow

1. **Freeze identity and decision.** Record host, parental line, construct,
   clone, bank, process, intended use, lifecycle stage, market and authority.
2. **Reconstruct provenance.** Trace origin, culture, manipulation, selection,
   cloning, adaptation, transfers and bank establishment; preserve gaps.
3. **Map construct evidence.** State structure/version, material, method,
   detection boundary and links to transcript and expressed-product evidence.
4. **Preserve clone rationale.** Record stages, attributes, process context,
   product evidence, trade-offs and discarded candidates.
5. **Control bank lifecycle.** Map parent-child genealogy, manufacture,
   storage, monitoring, inventory, shipment, thaw/use, excursions, replacement
   and retirement.
6. **Separate characterization questions.** State material, timing, method,
   capability, result, contradiction, limitation and reviewer for each.
7. **Assess stability through production age.** Integrate construct,
   phenotype, process performance, product attributes, analytical capability
   and exact culture-age traceability.
8. **Translate lifecycle change.** Link exact differences to validation,
   comparability, stability, filing, implementation and monitoring decisions.
9. **Qualify review.** Preserve evidence gaps, conditions, disagreements,
   residual risks, actions and authorized decisions.

## Required review roles

- cell-line development and molecular characterization;
- cell-bank manufacturing/operations and bank custodian;
- biosafety, microbiology and virology/viral safety as applicable;
- upstream process development/MSAT and manufacturing;
- analytical development, product-quality sciences and statistics;
- qualification/validation, quality unit and regulatory CMC;
- storage, logistics, data/inventory governance and site-change owners where
  applicable.

## Applicability and limitations

This package structures cell-substrate evidence and decision ownership. It does
not establish cell-line suitability, construct integrity, clone selection,
cell-bank release, purity, genetic or phenotypic stability, freedom from
adventitious agents, viral safety, production-age limits, process validation,
product comparability, regulatory acceptability, authorized bank use, batch
disposition or product release.

The applicable product, host, cell type, construct, bank, process, site, market,
filing and current source edition must be confirmed. The fictional example is
not a benchmark or recommended protocol.
