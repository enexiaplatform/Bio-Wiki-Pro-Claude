# Investigation, CAPA & Change Evidence Loop — Guide

Status: `editorial-reviewed` · Version: `1.0.0-review`

## Decision question

What evidence and accountable decisions are required to move a quality signal
from detection through investigation, action, effectiveness review and reusable
knowledge without losing lineage or overstating the conclusion?

## Applicability and do-not-use boundary

Use this pack to structure a bounded laboratory, process, data or quality-system
signal in API, drug product or protein-biologics work. Do not use it to invalidate
a result, assign root cause, approve CAPA or change control, accept effectiveness,
determine regulatory reporting, release a batch or generalize one case across
products and sites.

## Controlled source map

| Source ID | Use in this pack | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FDA-OOS-2022 | Laboratory assessment, full investigation and evidence questions | Does not supply a universal invalidation, retest, resample or disposition rule |
| FDA-DI-2018 | Data completeness, metadata, audit-trail and governance questions | Does not infer intent or replace the approved site investigation procedure |
| ICH-Q9-R1 | Risk-based scope, uncertainty and escalation | A risk score is not root-cause evidence or authorization |
| ICH-Q10 | CAPA, change management, effectiveness and knowledge-management connection | Does not approve an action, prove effectiveness or define regional reporting |

## Workflow

1. Freeze the signal, originating record, affected scope and decision owner.
2. Preserve immediate controls without converting containment into conclusion.
3. Separate observed facts, hypotheses, missing evidence and contradictory data.
4. Record the authorized investigation decision and its evidence basis.
5. Distinguish correction, corrective action, preventive action and change control.
6. Define an effectiveness question, measure, time window and accountable reviewer.
7. Record the effectiveness decision separately from activity completion.
8. Transfer only bounded, reviewed knowledge with applicability and limitations.

## Discovery questions

- Which exact record or signal triggered the review, and has its lineage been preserved?
- What product, process, method, system, period and site could be affected?
- Which evidence could refute the leading hypothesis or expand the scope?
- What decision will the effectiveness review make, beyond confirming task completion?
- Where may the learning be reused, and where must it explicitly not be generalized?

## Limitations

This pack supplies no default investigation phase, retest count, root-cause method,
risk threshold, CAPA due date, change category, effectiveness criterion or reporting
decision. Qualified site roles retain investigation, disposition, CAPA, change,
effectiveness, regulatory and release authority.
