# Investigation, CAPA & Change Evidence Loop

Version: `1.0.0-review`
Status: `editorial-reviewed`
Decision state: `Evidence required` or `Qualified review required` only

This working pack connects a quality signal to triage, investigation, correction,
CAPA, change control, effectiveness evidence and bounded knowledge transfer. It
keeps facts, hypotheses, decisions and authorizations separate across API, drug
product and protein-biologics contexts.

## Files

- `investigation-capa-change-evidence-loop-guide.md` — bounded evidence-to-action
  workflow.
- `investigation-capa-change-evidence-loop-blank.csv` — blank lineage register
  from signal through effectiveness and knowledge transfer.
- `investigation-capa-change-evidence-loop-fictional-example.md` — synthetic
  cross-product quality-system example with unresolved evidence.

## Controlled sources

`FDA-OOS-2022`, `FDA-DI-2018`, `ICH-Q9-R1`, `ICH-Q10`.

## Reviewer boundary

The accountable laboratory or process owner, quality unit, data-integrity or
system owner, relevant manufacturing-science role and regulatory CMC role must
review the exact record and intended action. Editorial review does not
constitute SME approval, investigation closure, CAPA approval, change approval,
effectiveness acceptance, batch disposition or controlled release.
