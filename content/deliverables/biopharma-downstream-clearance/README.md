# Biopharma Downstream Purification & Clearance Evidence Map - Start Here

This package helps a cross-functional Biopharma team connect product and
impurity targets, downstream unit operations, product-quality impact, viral
safety, recovery, process evidence, scale-down scope, controls, and lifecycle
triggers.

**Package version:** 1.0.0-review

**Review status:** editorial-reviewed

**Decision owner:** The authorized product, process, viral-safety, quality, and regulatory owners - not Atlas

## Package contents

1. `biopharma-downstream-clearance-guide.pdf` - decision workflow, clearance
   claim model, source basis, and review boundaries.
2. `biopharma-downstream-clearance-v1.xlsx` - blank formula-driven working file
   with validation, evidence register, attention flags, sources, and limitations.
3. `biopharma-downstream-clearance-v1-fictional-example.xlsx` - completed
   fictional CHO process example preserving unresolved resin, impurity,
   product-quality, and viral-safety questions.

## Recommended order

1. Confirm that the product and modality are within the package scope.
2. Frame one product/process decision in `Decision Brief`.
3. Define product, impurity, contaminant, safety, and recovery targets.
4. Map the actual unit-operation train and handoffs.
5. Write impurity, product-variant, viral, and recovery claims separately.
6. Record controlled evidence, scale-down scope, limitations, and contradictions.
7. Resolve every `Incomplete`, `Evidence required`, or `Review required` flag.
8. Obtain qualified process, analytical, viral-safety, quality, and regulatory review.
9. Transfer approved content into the site's controlled system.

## Important boundary

The package supplies no generic purification train. It supplies no generic operating range,
resin or membrane lifetime, impurity limit, viral-reduction factor, clearance
claim, validation conclusion, specification, or batch decision. Every
product-specific claim requires controlled provenance and qualified review.
