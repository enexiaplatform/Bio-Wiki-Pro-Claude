# Data Integrity Self-Check — Start Here

A structured, system-by-system self-assessment of your laboratory records
against ALCOA+ principles — paper, hybrid, and electronic — so you find and
fix data-integrity gaps before an inspector does. Built around FDA's Data
Integrity Q&A (2018), MHRA's GxP Data Integrity Guidance (2018), WHO TRS 996
Annex 5, and PIC/S PI 041-1 (2021), plus 21 CFR Part 11 and EU GMP Annex 11 /
Chapter 4.

## Who it's for

QC/QA professionals in pharma, biotech, and life-science laboratories: QC
supervisors running periodic self-inspections, QA auditors preparing for
regulatory inspections, and lab managers who inherit systems and need a fast,
evidence-based read on their data-integrity posture.

## What's inside

1. **Data Integrity Self-Check guide (PDF)** — the full working document:
   regulatory basis; an ALCOA+ deep dive (all nine principles, each with a
   paper example, an electronic example, and exact self-check questions);
   system inventory and risk-ranking template; paper record controls
   (notebooks, error correction, blank space, true copies); electronic
   controls (unique accounts, access privilege matrix, audit-trail capture
   and risk-based review, time synchronization, backup vs. archival); the
   hybrid-system "printout is not the original record" trap; spreadsheet
   controls with a compliant-spreadsheet checklist; laboratory hot spots
   (reprocessing, trial injections, manual integration, rounding, standalone
   equipment with no audit trail); behavioral/cultural checks; a scoring
   method and sampling plan; findings-to-CAPA workflow; what inspectors look
   for and the questions they actually ask; and a worked example of a
   self-check on a standalone HPLC with Empower.
2. **ALCOA+ Gap Log (CSV/Excel)** — a working findings register with columns
   for finding ID, date, system/record, ALCOA+ principle(s), description,
   evidence reference, risk class, root cause, corrective action, preventive
   action, owner, due date, effectiveness check, status, and verified by.
   Six realistic example rows show you how to fill it.

## Recommended order of use

1. Read guide sections 1–3 (regulatory basis, ALCOA+, scoping) and build your
   system inventory with risk ranking — this decides where you spend time.
2. Work the self-check system by system, highest risk first, using sections
   4–9 as your checklists and section 10 for scoring, sampling, evidence, and
   analyst interviews.
3. Log every observation and non-conformity in the Gap Log CSV; classify risk
   and drive CAPA per section 11, with effectiveness checks defined up front.
4. Before an inspection, re-run section 12 (483 themes and inspector
   interview questions) as a rehearsal with your team.

## Important

Educational tool and working template. It is not validated and not
regulator-approved. Verify every checkpoint against your approved SOPs, your
site quality system, current compendia and guidance editions, and your
validated system configurations. Risk classes, review frequencies, and
example values are clearly labeled examples — set your own site-approved
values. Not a substitute for QA approval or regulatory advice.
