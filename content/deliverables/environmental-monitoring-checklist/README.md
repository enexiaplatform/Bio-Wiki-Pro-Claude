# Environmental Monitoring Checklist — Start Here

A complete working kit for designing, running, and defending a risk-based
environmental monitoring (EM) program for classified cleanrooms and
controlled environments — aligned to EU GMP Annex 1 (2022), the FDA
aseptic processing guidance (2004), ISO 14644/14698, and USP <1116>.

Built for QC microbiologists, QA professionals, and manufacturing leads in
pharmaceutical, biotech, and life-science facilities who need an
inspection-ready EM program — not just a list of limits.

## What's inside

1. **Environmental Monitoring Program Guide (PDF)** — the main document.
   Eleven sections covering:
   - Regulatory basis and the Annex 1 grade limits table (A/B/C/D, air,
     settle plates, contact plates, glove prints)
   - Risk-based sample site selection and method choice (active air,
     settle, contact, swab, personnel)
   - Frequency guidance with an example schedule
   - Alert/action level setting and a full excursion response workflow
     with a printable investigation template
   - Organism identification policy and objectionable organisms
   - Trending and data review (per room, method, organism, operator)
   - Non-sterile / laboratory adaptation
   - Media, GPT, and incubation regimes
   - Common FDA 483 observations and inspector expectations
   - A complete worked excursion investigation (fictional Grade B
     Bacillus case)
   - An annual program review checklist with sign-off blocks
2. **EM Site & Sample Plan (CSV/Excel)** — a register template with six
   realistic example rows across grades A–D. Columns: room ID, room name,
   grade, classification reference, site ID, location description, method,
   frequency, at-rest/in-operation, alert level, action level,
   responsible person, qualification date. Replace the example rows with
   your own sites; keep one row per sample site.

## Recommended order of use

1. Read Sections 1–3 of the guide, then draft your site register in the
   CSV — this is the document an inspector asks for first.
2. Set your alert/action levels (Section 4) from your qualification and
   historical data, and adopt the excursion workflow and template.
3. Implement the ID policy (Section 5) and trending cadence (Section 6).
4. Use Section 9 before inspections and Section 11 once a year for the
   annual program review.

## Important

This pack is a general educational tool and working template. It is not
validated and not regulator-approved. Verify every limit, method, and
frequency against your approved SOPs, current compendial editions, your
contamination control strategy, and your site quality system. Site- and
product-specific limits must be set and approved by your QA. Nothing here
substitutes for QA approval, a validated method, or regulatory advice.
