# Culture Media Selection Guide — Start Here

A practical, audit-ready guide to choosing, preparing, qualifying, and
controlling microbiological culture media in a pharmaceutical QC laboratory —
so every plate and broth behind a reportable result is traceable to a
qualified, fit-for-purpose lot.

## Who this pack is for

- QC microbiology analysts who prepare, receive, and release media
- QC supervisors and QA reviewers who approve media lots and investigate
  Growth Promotion Testing (GPT) failures
- Anyone preparing for an FDA/EMA/MHRA inspection who needs to defend media
  control as a system

## What's inside

1. **Culture Media Selection Guide** (`culture-media-selection-guide.md`,
   rendered to PDF) — the full working document:
   - Regulatory basis: USP <61>/<62>/<71>, <1117>, <1231>, Ph. Eur.
     2.6.1/2.6.12/2.6.13, ISO 11133, FDA cGMP expectations, EU GMP Annex 1
   - Media selection by test: TSA/SCDA, SDA, FTM, TSB, MacConkey, Mannitol
     Salt, Cetrimide, XLD, Rappaport Vassiliadis broth, CLED, R2A, and
     neutralizers for antimicrobial products and disinfectant efficacy
   - Dehydrated vs ready-to-use media and the QC implications of each
   - Media preparation controls: water quality, weighing, pH before/after
     sterilization, sterilization cycle validation (overheating and the F0
     concept), pouring at 45-50 C, storage, and expiry assignment
   - Full GPT procedure: compendial strains per medium, <=100 cfu inoculum,
     acceptance criteria (including inhibitory and indicative properties),
     GPT frequency, and fertility/sterility checks for sterility-test media
   - Plate QC, sterility checks, equivalence studies for supplier/RTU
     changes, storage and transport validation
   - Common failure modes (GPT failure, pH drift, overheating, desiccation)
     with causes and fixes
   - What an inspector will ask, and a complete worked example of receiving
     and qualifying a new lot of TSA — including a handled GPT failure
2. **Media & GPT Log** (`media-gpt-log.csv`, opens in Excel) — a lot-release
   register with columns for lot, supplier, CoA review, prep and
   sterilization data, pH before/after, appearance, test strains, inoculum
   cfu, incubation, recovery, inhibitory/indicative results, pass/fail,
   expiry, and release sign-off. Six realistic example rows are included
   (five passes across TSA, SDA, FTM, MacConkey, and Cetrimide, plus one
   rejected TSA batch with an overheated cycle and failed recovery) so you
   can see exactly how to fill it — replace them with your own data.

## Recommended order of use

1. Read the guide end to end once, mapping each section to your current
   media SOPs.
2. Localize the fill-in fields (site water spec, expiry limits, storage
   rules) and route the localized version through your document control and
   QA approval.
3. Start logging every media lot and batch in the CSV from the next receipt
   onward — quarantine until GPT passes, then release.
4. Use Section 9 (common failures) when a GPT or sterility check fails, and
   Section 10 (inspector expectations) to self-audit before an inspection.
