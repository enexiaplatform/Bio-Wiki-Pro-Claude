# Decision-led Statistics & Process Evidence Map — Guide

Status: `editorial-reviewed` · Version: `1.0.0-review`

## Decision question

What data, measurement, design, analysis and uncertainty evidence is needed
before a statistical or multivariate result can support the named process or
quality decision?

## Applicability and do-not-use boundary

Use this pack for a bounded process-development, monitoring, investigation or
change question with named data and accountable reviewers. Do not use it to
declare causality from association, infer a CPP, set universal thresholds,
approve a model, establish process capability or replace validation and quality
decisions.

## Controlled source map

| Source ID | Use in this pack | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ICH-Q8-R2 | Product/process understanding and development evidence | Does not define product-specific CQAs, CPPs or ranges |
| ICH-Q9-R1 | Decision-focused risk and uncertainty review | A risk score is not evidence of control |
| FDA-PROCESS-VALIDATION-2011 | Lifecycle process evidence and continued verification context | Does not prescribe a universal sampling or statistical rule |
| FDA-DI-2018 | Data lineage, completeness and governance questions | Does not replace site data-governance procedures |
| ICH-Q10 | Knowledge, change and management-review connection | Does not approve implementation or effectiveness |

## Workflow

1. Freeze the decision, scope, response and permitted use of the analysis.
2. Version the dataset, exclusions, transformations, source systems and owner.
3. Review measurement-system capability before interpreting process variation.
4. Match the design or analysis to the data structure and decision question.
5. Record assumptions, diagnostics, uncertainty and contradictory observations.
6. Separate association, prediction, causal evidence and operational control.
7. Define lifecycle monitoring, invalidation triggers and accountable next action.

## Discovery questions

- Which decision could change because of this analysis?
- Are the data and measurement system comparable across time, scale and site?
- Which assumptions or exclusions materially affect the conclusion?
- What evidence would challenge or invalidate the proposed model use?

## Limitations

This pack supplies no default sample size, significance rule, model metric,
control limit, capability target or acceptance threshold. Qualified statistical,
domain, data-integrity and quality review remains required.
